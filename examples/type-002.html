Why the agent drifts like this

Size is set per component, and "fit more content" wins every time: 12–13px body, full-width containers, five one-off sizes for headings, labels, notes and links. The result reads dense, loses hierarchy and makes every paragraph feel equally (un)important.

The repair

Two declarations fix most of it: a reading measure of roughly 60–70 characters and a five-step scale (12 / 15 / 20 / 28 / 40 of your base unit). Map each text role to one scale step. Density returns, and headings lead again.

Find it in your project

Paste your CSS into Guard Lite with a font-sizes = contract line — PPG-TYPE-002 lists every literal size that is not on the scale.
