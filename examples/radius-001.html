Why the agent drifts like this

Radius is bound to vibes: "friendly" pulls 999px, "modern" pulls 22px, "secure" pulls sharp corners. The agent applies the vibe per element instead of choosing one turn language for the whole screen. Mixing pill, square and heavy turns on one card fails at a glance.

The repair

Declare a short radius scale (for example 0 / 4px / 8px / 16px) and assign by component size: inputs and buttons get the small turn, cards get the medium one, oversized displays get the large one, and only avatars and indicators earn the full circle — deliberately.

Find it in your project

Paste your CSS into Guard Lite with a radii = contract line — PPG-RADIUS-001 lists every literal radius outside the set.
