Why the agent drifts like this

Agents reason element-by-element and treat colour as free decoration. The badge asked to "stand out", so it got teal. The progress bar got pink. Nothing checks the totals. Six accents means zero accents — the eye has nowhere to land first.

The repair

Declare a one-accent palette with a neutral scale, then express emphasis hierarchically: the primary action holds the accent, secondary actions hold neutrals, status appears through labels and weight. Every rendered hue traces back to a named token.

Find it in your project

Paste your CSS into Guard Lite with a colour contract of two or three approved values — PPG-COLOR-001 lists every literal hex outside the set.
